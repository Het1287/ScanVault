# 📄 ScanVault

**A private, offline-first document scanner for Android.**
Scan, organize, lock and back up your documents, with your files staying in your hands.

## 📥 Download

Get the latest APK from the [**Releases page**](https://github.com/Het1287/ScanVault/releases/latest).

**How to install**
1. Download the `.apk` file on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap **Install**, then open ScanVault.

## ✨ Features

### Scanning and editing
- 📷 Document scanning powered by **Google ML Kit**
- 🎨 Scan filters: B&W, grayscale, magic color, contrast
- ✂️ Built-in photo editor with crop
- ✍️ Signatures and annotations
- 🔤 OCR with **auto-naming** of documents from detected text

### Organization
- 📁 Folders, ⭐ favorites and 🏷️ tags
- 🔎 Full-text **OCR search**
- ↕️ Sort options and ☑️ multi-select bulk actions
- 🗑️ Trash with 30-day restore
- ⏰ Expiry reminders for time-sensitive documents

### Privacy and security
- 🔒 Folder lock and file lock
- 🔐 Password-protected PDF export
- 💾 Local **encrypted backup and restore**
- ☁️ Optional Google Drive backup, stored in your own app-private Drive space
- 🏠 Documents are stored privately in app storage

### Convenience
- 📤 Share target: import images and PDFs from other apps
- 🧩 Home-screen widget and scanner shortcut
- 🌗 Themes and accent colors
- 💬 Feedback option in Settings

---

## 🔐 Privacy

- No ads, no trackers.
- Documents stay on your device unless you choose to back them up.
- Cloud backup goes to **your own** Google account only.
