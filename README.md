# 📄 ScanVault

**Your documents, your phone, your control.**

ScanVault is a free Android app that turns your phone into a private document scanner. Scan papers, organize them, lock them, edit your PDFs, and back them up, all without handing your files to anyone.

## 💡 Why ScanVault?

Many scanner apps push you to upload documents to their cloud, show ads, or ask for a subscription. ScanVault keeps your documents on your own phone. Backup is optional, and it only goes to your own Google account.

## 📥 Download and Install

**Latest version: v2.1.0**

👉 [**Click here to download the latest version**](https://github.com/Het1287/ScanVault/releases/latest)

1. Open the link above **on your Android phone**.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the file ending in **.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file.
4. If your phone asks, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
5. Tap **Install**, then open **ScanVault**.

> ⚠️ **Seeing a Play Protect warning?** ScanVault is safe to install. Android shows this warning for any app that is not downloaded from the Play Store. Tap **More details** and then **Install anyway**.

## ✨ What can it do?

### Scan and edit
- 📷 Scan documents with your camera, one page or many at once (batch scanning)
- 🖼️ Import photos from your gallery
- 🎨 Make scans clear with Black & White, Grayscale, Magic Color, and Contrast filters
- ✂️ Crop and edit photos inside the app
- ✍️ Add your signature or notes to a document
- 🔤 Automatically read text from scans (OCR) and use it to name your documents

### PDF tools
- 📖 Built-in PDF reader to open and read PDFs right inside the app
- 🗜️ Compress big PDFs to make them smaller and easier to share
- 🔗 Merge several PDFs into one
- 💧 Add a watermark to protect your documents
- 🖼️ Convert PDF to images
- 📄 Convert images to PDF

### Edit your PDFs
Open any PDF from the Files section and you can:
- 🔄 Rotate pages
- ↕️ Reorder pages
- 🗑️ Remove pages you don't need
- ✂️ Copy or pull out pages to create a brand new PDF

### Stay organized
- 📁 Put documents in folders, mark favorites ⭐, and add tags 🏷️
- 🔎 Search for any word inside your scans and imported PDFs
- ☑️ Select many documents and act on them at once
- 🗑️ Deleted something by mistake? Restore it from Trash within 30 days
- ⏰ Get reminders when a document is about to expire
- 📤 Share any document as a PDF or as images

### Keep it private
- 🔒 Lock folders and files
- 🔐 Save PDFs with a password
- 💾 Make an encrypted backup on your phone and restore it any time
- ☁️ Optional Google Drive backup
- 🏠 Your documents are stored privately inside the app

### Everyday convenience
- 📥 Send images and PDFs to ScanVault from other apps
- 🧩 Home-screen widget and quick scanner shortcut
- 🌗 Light and dark themes with accent colors
- 💬 Send feedback straight from Settings

## 🔐 Privacy

- 🚫 No ads and no trackers
- 📊 No analytics and no crash-reporting tools
- 📱 Your documents stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up
- ☁️ Cloud backup goes only to your own Google account
- 📜 The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are inside the app under **Settings → Support**
